Nutshell – Privacy policy

by pejlar · last updated 16 November 2026

Nutshell is a collection of puzzle and arcade games for Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, made by pejlar. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play. The short version: pejlar does not collect any personal data through the app, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers in it.

Who is responsible

Nutshell is published by Pejlar AB (“pejlar”), Sweden. Pejlar AB is responsible for this policy. You can reach us at support@pejlar.se.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, saved games), statistics, high scores, achievements, settings and whether you own the Golden Nut are stored only on your device. They are never sent to pejlar. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Purchases (the Golden Nut)

Nutshell has one optional one-time purchase, the Golden Nut. It is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment under their own terms and privacy policies. pejlar never sees your payment details. The store may give pejlar basic order information, such as an order number, the country and the amount, which pejlar uses only for accounting, refunds and support.

Before a purchase, the app asks a simple grown-up question so that children can't buy by accident.

Google Play Games (optional, Android)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the app uses Google Play Games Services to show online leaderboards and achievements and to keep a backup of your progress in your Google account. For this, Google processes your Play Games player profile (player id and name), the scores and achievements you earn, and the backup of your game progress. pejlar does not receive this information.

This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in the Google Play Games settings. You can use every game without signing in.

Ratings

Now and then, after a win, the app may ask Google Play or the App Store to show their own rating window. Any rating or review you leave goes to the store under its own policy; the app is not told whether you rated.

What pejlar does not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
  • No account with pejlar, and no data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No chat or contact with other players.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The app only uses the internet for Google Play Games (if you sign in) and for purchases through the store. Everything else works offline.

If you email us

If you contact pejlar, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

Children

Nutshell does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no ads and no chat, and purchases are behind a grown-up question. Optional Google Play Games sign-in follows Google's own rules for accounts.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you may ask what personal data pejlar holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. As the app sends nothing to pejlar, this normally only concerns emails you have sent us. Write to support@pejlar.se. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
