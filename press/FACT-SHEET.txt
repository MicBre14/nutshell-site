ONE LINE
30 puzzle and arcade games in one small app – and no ads, ever.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Nutshell is 30 puzzle, card and arcade games in one app, made by pejlar, a tiny Swedish
studio. It has no ads, no account and no chat, and it works offline. Twelve games are free;
a single one-time purchase, the Golden Nut, unlocks the other 18 and the two hardest
difficulties.

THE STORY
Nutshell started from a simple frustration: "I'm tired of games that are more ads than game."
Many puzzle apps show a video after every level, nag you to buy coins, or ask you to watch an
ad to continue. pejlar wanted the opposite – one app with all the games you'd normally install
separately, where you just play.

THE GAMES
Free: Arrows, Sudoku, Pixel Picture, Pipe Link, Color Sort, Block Fit, Tile Match, Stack Drop,
Double Up, Nut Smash, Solitaire (several ways to play), Nut Mahjong.
With the Golden Nut: Acorn Ascent, Before the Frost, Crowns, Nut Escape, Minefield,
Nuts & Bolts, Bridges, Bubble Pop, Squirrel Stash, Trees & Squirrels, Happy Squirrels,
Nut Sums, Code Nut, Squirrel Run, Fence It, Memory Nuts, Acorn Flip, Lanterns.

FEATURES
- No ads, no chat, no loot boxes, no energy timers
- A daily challenge in every free game, and in all 30 with the Golden Nut
- Five difficulties, from "Easy peasy" to "Are you nuts?!"
- Endless modes, including an endless climb in Acorn Ascent
- Memory Nuts party mode for up to four players on one phone or tablet
- A grown-up question before the purchase, so nothing is bought by accident
- Nutshell sends nothing to pejlar; progress stays on the device. Optional Google Play Games
  sign-in shares scores and achievements with Google.
