The story

“I'm tired of games that are more ads than game.”

Nutshell started from that frustration. Many puzzle apps show a video after every level, nag you to buy coins, or ask you to watch an ad to continue. pejlar, a tiny Swedish studio, wanted the opposite: one small app with all the games you would normally install one by one – where you just play.

The result is 30 games in one app, from Sudoku, Solitaire and Mahjong to original arcade games like Acorn Ascent and Before the Frost. Twelve games are free, with no ads. A single one-time purchase, the Golden Nut, unlocks the other 18 and the two hardest difficulties. No subscription, no coins.

Ready-to-use descriptions

Short: Nutshell is 30 puzzle and arcade games in one small app – and no ads, ever.

Nutshell is 30 puzzle, card and arcade games in one app, made by pejlar, a tiny Swedish studio. It has no ads, no account and no chat, and it works offline. Play Sudoku, Solitaire, Nut Mahjong and nine more games for free, with a new daily challenge in each. A single one-time purchase, the Golden Nut, unlocks 18 more games – including the arcade climber Acorn Ascent and the autumn adventure Before the Frost – plus the two hardest difficulties.

Features

  • No ads, no chat, no loot boxes, no energy timers
  • A daily challenge in every free game – in all 30 with the Golden Nut
  • Five difficulties, from 🙂 Easy peasy to 🥜 Are you nuts?!
  • Endless modes, including an endless climb in Acorn Ascent
  • Memory Nuts party mode for up to four players on one phone or tablet
  • A grown-up question before the purchase, so nothing is bought by accident
  • Nutshell sends nothing to pejlar – progress stays on the device. Optional Google Play Games sign-in shares scores with Google. Privacy policy
